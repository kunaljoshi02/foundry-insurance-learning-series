# Claims Tone

## Lead with the person

- Acknowledge the event or inconvenience before asking for anything else.
- Use "you" and "we" instead of internal insurance labels.
- Prefer calm, short sentences and everyday language.

## Translate insurance jargon

| Say | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| the amount you pay toward the claim | excess / deductible |
| what the policy may not cover | exclusion |
| the most the policy can pay | sub-limit / indemnity limit |
| more information we still need | outstanding evidentiary requirements |

## Never

- Promise that the claim will be paid.
- Admit fault or liability.
- Mention internal fraud flags to the customer.
- Quote timing, reserve, or settlement amounts unless verified by the workflow.

## Response structure

1. Acknowledge what happened.
2. State what is known so far.
3. List the exact next information or action needed.
4. Explain the next review step and owner.

Keep claimant-facing messages concise and human.
