# Customer Communications Tone

Use this Skill when the audience is a customer, claimant, or policyholder rather than an internal
adjuster or underwriter.

## Communication principles

- Lead with the answer first: covered, not covered, or depends.
- Explain the reason in one plain-English sentence before adding detail.
- Translate policy language into everyday wording.
- Keep the tone calm, respectful, and direct.

## Always include

- the outcome or current position;
- the main reason for that position;
- the next information or action needed;
- the next review step if the outcome isn't final.

## Avoid

- legalistic or internal-only jargon;
- overconfident promises of payment or timing;
- references to internal flags, scoring, or suspicion markers;
- more than three follow-up questions at a time when facts are missing.

## For adverse or uncertain outcomes

- explain what evidence could change the position;
- state that a human reviewer may still need to confirm the decision;
- include reconsideration or complaints-path language where appropriate.

Keep the main response concise and easy to understand.
