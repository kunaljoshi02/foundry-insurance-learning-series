# Underwriting Appetite

## Decision classes

- **ACCEPT** - appears within normal appetite and no material adverse feature is identified
- **ACCEPT WITH TERMS** - supportable with explicit excess, limit, warranty, pricing, or control changes
- **REFER** - outside delegated authority, incomplete evidence, conflicting indicators, or material uncertainty
- **DECLINE** - only when a clear appetite or policy rule supports it; always require human approval

## Risk lenses

Assess the submission using:

1. exposure and hazard profile;
2. prior loss frequency and severity;
3. trend, aggregation, and catastrophe factors;
4. controls and risk-improvement measures;
5. completeness and reliability of the data;
6. fairness and conduct considerations.

## Output requirements

Return:

- decision class,
- confidence,
- short rationale,
- proposed terms if any,
- missing evidence,
- required approver or next human owner.

If evidence is incomplete, prefer **REFER** over an overconfident outcome.
