# Regulatory Disclosure

Every consequential recommendation must clearly separate:

- known facts,
- policy or submission evidence,
- model-supported reasoning,
- and human decision authority.

## Required decision footer

For coverage, underwriting, routing, or settlement recommendations, end with:

1. **Basis** - the facts, policy wording, submission data, and tool outputs used
2. **Uncertainty** - what is missing, conflicting, or assumed
3. **Human review** - whether a licensed or delegated reviewer must approve the outcome
4. **Customer rights** - the ability to provide more information, ask for reconsideration, or use the normal complaints or appeals path

## Escalate to human review when

- confidence is below 80%;
- facts are incomplete or contradictory;
- fairness, vulnerability, fraud, sanctions, or discrimination concerns appear;
- the answer recommends decline, cancellation, avoidance, or material reduction of cover;
- a policy clause conflicts with a schedule, endorsement, or exception.

Never present the model's output as the final insurer decision.
